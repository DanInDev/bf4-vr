## Learned

- `unlockfind` and `givegun` know no grips in Shanghai. The grip assets come only with the weapon crate's guns, and the
  checkpoint's own UMP-45 has one.
- The crate opens headless with the use reach, but its menu never closes again (only a restart does).
- After a load the slot captures can leave the grenade or knife in hand, so check `holster` slot= before any give.
- A fake controller's palm sits 6 cm behind it.
- Esc right after the load skips the Shanghai briefing.
- All five are now in CLAUDE.md.
- Verifier lessons: a probe whose result holds by construction proves nothing (#91's 0.0 cm). A rule must be written as
  it is applied (#141). Un-dropping a state affects every reader of that state (#136's `gun_dropped()` callers).
