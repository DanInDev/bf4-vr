    model's is closed).

  - The red square on iron sights and holos is gone.
- **Weapons (#89, #87, #88, #90, #94, #95, #97, #98):**
  - Fast guns no longer start the game's own reload.
  - The rack fist sits on the knob, and the 11 guns whose reload never pulls the handle got handle points from the model.
  - Magazines are held at their own depth.
  - Pump guns: no game pump stroke after a shot, and the shell sits across the fingers.
  - Belt-fed: the hand stays on the feed cover, and an empty gun has no belt. The belt that dropped at once is fixed
    for the gun-in-off-hand case; it was not reproduced live.
  - The FAMAS's and AK-12's handle racks from both sides.
  - The P90's and AK 5C's magazines are pulled out by hand.
- Every new behaviour has a live setting (panel, ini). The night branch is deployed for the headset.
